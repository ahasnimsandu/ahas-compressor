The honest part

Not every file can shrink.

JPEG, MP4, encrypted data, and existing archives may barely compress — or grow slightly. Media Optimizer trades detail for size, but even it cannot guarantee a target ratio.

50 GB → 50 MB only works for extremely repetitive data. macOS extended metadata and resource forks are not preserved by the archive mode; links/special files are skipped and reported.
